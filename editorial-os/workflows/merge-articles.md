# Workflow: Merge Articles

**Purpose:** consolidate pages that serve the same reader intent.
**Destructive actions:** none without explicit approval. This workflow produces an
analysis, not a merge.

## Trigger
Two or more pages appear to answer the same question. Similar topics are **not** a
trigger — `skills/content-consolidation/SKILL.md` explains the distinction.

## Produce a MERGE ANALYSIS containing
1. **Source pages** — URLs, titles, dates, word counts
2. **Intent comparison** — each page's intent in the reader's words; same, adjacent
   or different
3. **Unique information** — what exists only on each page
4. **Duplicate information** — what is repeated
5. **Proposed destination** — which URL survives, and why
6. **URL implications** — what redirects would be needed
7. **Internal link implications** — every inbound link to a retiring URL, listed
8. **Content to preserve** — explicitly
9. **Content to discard** — explicitly, with justification
10. **Redirect recommendation**
11. **Pre-implementation checks** — traffic, backlinks, indexation status
12. **Confidence level**

## Then stop
Present the analysis to the Editor-in-Chief in Portuguese and wait. Merging,
deleting and redirecting all require explicit approval.

## Never
Merge to reduce article count. Discard information that exists nowhere else. Retire a
URL without a redirect plan.
