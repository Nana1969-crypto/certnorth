# Workflow: Update Article

**Purpose:** keep an otherwise good article accurate.
**Scope:** factual currency only. Structural change is a rebuild, not an update.

## Check
- Current official prices and fees, **for the relevant region**
- Exam requirements and formats
- Eligibility rules
- Renewal requirements
- Statistics and their publication dates
- Changed policies or terminology
- Broken or redirected source links
- Internal links to articles that have since changed

## Sequence
1. List every volatile claim in the article with its current source and date
2. Re-verify each against the owning body
3. Update **only** what evidence supports
4. Where a figure cannot be re-verified, soften it to match the available evidence
   rather than leaving false precision — or mark it UNVERIFIED and report
5. Add any source that now supports a claim better
6. Record what changed
7. Set `updatedAt` to the date of the change — never as decoration
8. Portuguese brief if the change alters a recommendation; otherwise a short note

## Rule
An update that changes no content changes no date.
