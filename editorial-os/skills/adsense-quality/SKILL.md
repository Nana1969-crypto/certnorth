# Skill: AdSense and Publisher Quality

## What this skill is not
It is not a method for obtaining AdSense approval. **No editorial change guarantees
approval.** There is no article length and no article count that produces it.
Anyone claiming otherwise is guessing.

What this skill does is reduce genuine publisher-quality risk and increase real user
value, using Google's published quality and publisher-policy principles as guidance.

## Risk categories
1. **Thin content** — little substantive publisher content on the page
2. **Insufficient original value** — the page adds nothing beyond its sources
3. **Repetitive pages** — the site reads as mass-produced
4. **Unfinished pages** — placeholders, empty sections, broken elements
5. **Misleading claims** — including false precision and overstated certainty
6. **Deceptive or unverifiable disclosures** — the highest-severity category
7. **Auto-generated content without meaningful editorial review**
8. **Poor user experience**
9. **Excessive commercial emphasis** relative to informational value
10. **Unclear editorial identity** — who publishes this, and on what basis

## Assess two levels
Assess the page, then assess the **site-wide pattern the page contributes to**. A
strong page inside a uniform site still carries the site's risk. This is currently
CertNorth's main exposure.

## Disclosure rule
Every disclosure must describe verifiable reality. Claiming affiliate commissions
without affiliate links is a deceptive disclosure even when the intent was routine.
Escalate any unverifiable disclosure to `reports/DISCLOSURE_AUDIT.md` — do not
quietly rewrite it, and never invent a relationship to make a disclosure true.

## Reporting
Severity, evidence, and a specific remedy per risk. No predictions about approval.

## Never
Recommend padding, or publishing to hit a number. State or imply that a change will
secure approval. Treat a rejection reason as known when it has not been supplied.
