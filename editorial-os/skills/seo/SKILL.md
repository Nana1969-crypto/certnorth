# Skill: SEO

## Principle
Optimise for the reader. Every technique below is either something that helps a
person find and use the page, or it does not belong here.

## Checklist
- **Intent** — state in one sentence what the reader wants; confirm the article
  delivers exactly that
- **Title** — accurate, specific, honours what the page contains; ≤60 characters for
  `metaTitle`, unique site-wide
- **H1** — matches the promise of the title
- **Headings** — describe real sections; a reader should be able to navigate by them
- **Meta description** — ≤155 characters, unique, describes the page honestly
- **Internal links** — at least one outbound with descriptive anchor text; and plan
  inbound links from existing pages
- **Canonical** — self-referencing
- **Structured data** — appropriate to the content type, never claiming more than the
  page contains
- **Topical fit** — strengthens a cluster rather than fragmenting it
- **Cannibalisation** — no other page targets this intent

## Inbound links are not optional
The current site has **9 orphaned articles** with zero inbound internal links. New
articles link outward to old ones; nobody goes back to link forward. Every article
brief must name the existing pages that will link to it, and those edits must ship.

## Anchor text
Descriptive and natural: "how PDUs actually work", not "click here" and not a bare
keyword string.

## Never
Keyword stuff. Create headings to host keywords. Add irrelevant terms. Build a page
whose only purpose is targeting a query. Write a title the article cannot honour.
