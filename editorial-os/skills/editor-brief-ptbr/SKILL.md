# Skill: Editor Brief (pt-BR)

## Purpose
Convert all research and analysis into a short Portuguese briefing the
Editor-in-Chief can decide from — without needing to be a subject-matter expert.

## Audience
Portuguese-speaking, not a specialist in project management, SEO, research
methodology or Google's systems. She needs the decision, the reasoning, and the risks
— not the raw analysis.

## Required sections

### O QUE DESCOBRIMOS
Main findings, in plain language. Lead with the most decision-relevant one.

### FATOS IMPORTANTES
Verified facts, each with its source named. Mark anything **NÃO VERIFICADO** clearly.

### O QUE OS DADOS MOSTRAM
Interpretation. What the evidence points to, and how strongly.

### O QUE NÃO PODEMOS AFIRMAR
Limitations. What we could not verify, where sources disagree, what the data cannot
prove. **Never omit this section** — if it is empty, the analysis is incomplete.

### NOSSA CONCLUSÃO EDITORIAL
What CertNorth should tell readers, and why.

### RISCOS
Factual, editorial, SEO or publisher-quality concerns, with severity.

### FONTES PRINCIPAIS
The three to five sources that carry the argument, with what each supports.

### O QUE PRECISA DA SUA APROVAÇÃO
A short, explicit list of decisions only she can make. State the default if she
approves and the consequence if she declines.

## Rules
- Portuguese, plain, no jargon without a one-line explanation
- Explain trade-offs rather than presenting a single option as inevitable
- Never bury a limitation
- Say plainly when the honest recommendation is *not to publish*
- Keep it short enough to read in a few minutes; detail belongs in the reports
