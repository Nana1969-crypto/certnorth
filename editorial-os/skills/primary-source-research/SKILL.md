# Skill: Primary Source Research

## When to use
Any time a factual claim will appear in a CertNorth article.

## Rule
Go to the body that owns the fact. PMI owns PMP fees. Scrum.org owns PSM details.
The Scrum Guide owns what Scrum requires. BLS owns US occupational medians. A blog
summarising any of these is not a source for it.

## Procedure
1. Identify who owns the fact.
2. Find that body's own page or document for it.
3. Record the exact statement, the URL, and the document's date.
4. Record how you obtained it — the document itself, or a search-result summary of
   it. **These are not the same and must be distinguished.**
5. If the owning body's page cannot be reached, mark the fact **UNVERIFIED** and
   record what was attempted.

## Known access limitation in this environment
Direct fetching of external pages may be blocked. Where a fact was established from
search-result summaries of an official page rather than from the page itself, say so
in the dossier. Do not describe it as verified at source.

## Regional pricing trap
Some bodies price by country. PMI shows fees in the local currency of the account's
region, and those values do not convert cleanly from the US figures. Never present a
US price as universal; label the region.

## Red flags
- A figure that appears everywhere but traces to no primary source
- A training provider's claim about its own certification
- A statistic with no stated methodology or date
- A number repeated across SEO sites — repetition is not verification

## Output
Facts recorded in the Research Dossier, each with source, date and acquisition method.
