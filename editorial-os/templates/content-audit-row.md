**Depth is not word count.** A 600-word article that fully answers its question is
adequate; a 1,400-word article restating a source is thin.

**🔴 REMOVE** means *recommended for removal after further validation* — traffic,
backlinks, indexation and inbound links must be checked first. It is never
authorisation to delete.
